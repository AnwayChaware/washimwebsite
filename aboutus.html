About Our Handball Club

Shree Sant Gajanan Maharaj Handball Club Karanja (Lad), Washim is a premier sports institution dedicated to promoting the sport of handball across Washim District and Maharashtra state. Registered under Regt. MAHARASHTRA 383/2013 F 7817 WASHIM, the club works tirelessly to identify, train, and empower aspiring youth in sports.

Affiliated with the Handball Association of Maharashtra and the Handball Federation of India (HFI), our organization provides state-of-the-art coaching, professional infrastructure, and competition opportunities for players across all age categories — from Sub-Junior and Junior to Senior National levels.

Key Objectives & Focus Areas

  • Grassroots Development: Training school and college students in fundamental handball skills and fitness.
  • Inclusivity & Empowerment: Inspiring players of every gender, age group, and rural background to excel on national and state platforms.
  • Excellence & Discipline: Cultivating sportsmanship, leadership, and physical conditioning through regular coaching camps.
  • National Representation: Nurturing talented athletes to represent Maharashtra and India in national championships.

Club Leadership & Guidance

Under the leadership of President Shree Devendra Patil Tathode, Vice President Shree Vijay Patil Kale, Treasurer Shree Darshan Rokade, Secretary Shree Rahul Gawande, and Technical Head Engr Anway Amit Chaware, our institution continues to achieve milestones at state and national tournaments.
