ABOUT US

The Handball Association Of Washim District is affiliated with the Handball Association of Maharashtra and Handball Federation of India.

This is the official home of handball on Google, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter. Discover news, training programs, player achievements, and upcoming tournaments.

Our vision is to succeed as a leading sports body that captivates and inspires players across all age groups and communities.
