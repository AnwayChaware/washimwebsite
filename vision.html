Our Primary Vision

Our main vision is to succeed as a leading global sport that will captivate and inspire people of every age group, gender, and rural community through handball. We aim to establish Washim District as a powerhouse of handball talent in Maharashtra and across India.

Our Mission

To foster physical fitness, teamwork, and athletic excellence by providing world-class coaching, organizing district and state championships, and creating pathways for youth from all socio-economic backgrounds to reach National and International arenas.

Core Values & Pillars

🏆 Athletic Excellence

Rigorous training programs led by experienced coaches to prepare players for state and national competitions.

🤝 Youth Inclusion

Providing equal opportunities for boys and girls from rural and underprivileged communities to participate in competitive sports.

🎖️ Integrity & Sportsmanship

Instilling values of fair play, discipline, leadership, and respect both on and off the court.

🌟 Community Engagement

Building a supportive ecosystem involving schools, parents, coaches, and sports enthusiasts to promote a healthy lifestyle.
